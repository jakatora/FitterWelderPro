### Description — limit 4000 — 3412/4000 znaków

```text
Cut lists from isometric dimensions, elbow take-outs, rolling offsets, saddles, pressure tests and a weld log in one app. FitterWelder Pro is a calculator for industrial pipe fitters and welders: in the fabrication shop, on site and up on the scaffold.

Calculators, projects and the weld log work without an internet connection. Internet is only needed for the chat.

FOR PIPE FITTERS
• Cut list from isometric dimensions: ISO dimension minus the take-outs of elbows, tees, reducers, flanges and valves from ASME B16.9, B16.5 and B16.10 tables, with weld gap and a dimension reference point (centre or face).
• Enter dimensions in millimetres or inches (76" or 4 1/2"), even as a sum such as 3000+525-80. Cut lengths are shown in millimetres.
• Stock-length nesting with saw kerf and labelled pieces, bill of materials, cut list export to PDF and CSV.
• ISO notebook: isometric sketch pad with fittings, valves and weld numbers, CUT length from deductions, sketch autosave, PDF export.
• Elbows: LR/SR 90° and 45° centre-to-face, cutting an elbow to any angle, elbow roll in % and degrees, insert between elbows.
• Rolling offset with roll angle, two-elbow route, pipe fall (%, mm/m, degrees), slope cut, thermal expansion.
• Saddles: fish-mouth table for a 90° branch and a printable 1:1 wrap-around template for 15–90° branches.
• Reducer trimming, weld preparation (EN ISO 9692), pipe weight.
• Tables: DN / NPS / OD, wall thickness by schedule with kg/m, sanitary tube to ASME BPE and DIN 11850.
• Pipe supports (spacing to MSS SP-69), flange bolt torque, unit converter (mm, in, ft, °C/°F, bar/MPa/psi, gas flow).
• Photos of heat numbers and material certificates stored with each project.

FOR WELDERS
• Heat input calculator: EN formula with thermal efficiency η and the ASME formula without η.
• Carbon equivalent (CE) and preheat (Seferian method, with the B31.3 minimum).
• Shielding gas use and cylinder count, purge time to a target O2 level, timer.
• Pressure test to B31.3 (with ST/S), B31.1, EN 13480 / PED and ASME VIII, with a PDF test report.
• Pipe welding parameters (amps, gases, approved and your own), tank parameters, orbital TIG for food and pharma, tungsten selection.
• Weld journal (weld log) with PDF and CSV export, test coupon log, pre-weld checklist.
• Stainless: heat tint colours to AWS D18.2, pickling and passivation.

COMMUNITY CHAT
A public chat for pipe fitters and welders with topic rooms: welding, pipe fitting, jobs and crews. Messages are visible to everyone and signed with the first and last name from your profile. You can report messages and block users. The chat needs an internet connection.

ON THE JOB
• Tool search, pinned favourites and recently used tools.
• One-file backup of projects, weld journal and photos, with restore on another phone.
• Built-in help with search.
• App language: English or Polish, switchable in the app (pełny interfejs po polsku).
• No ads.

YOUR DATA
Projects, calculations, the weld journal and photos stay on your phone. Only chat data goes to our server: the message text, your first and last name and a random installation ID. You can delete your data from your profile.

Results are a working aid. Always check them against the project documents, the WPS and the applicable standards. Standard names (ASME, AWS, EN, DIN, MSS) identify the source of tables and formulas; the app is not affiliated with these organisations.
```
